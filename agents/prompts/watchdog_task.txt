## Expected process

The steps this run is expected to run, in order. This is a map, not a gate:
nothing rejects a step for being absent from it. Compare each incoming
begin_step's step_id against it -- one that is not here is unplanned, and must
be named as unplanned in whatever you record. contract_clause_groups records
where in the process each group of clauses is judged; effects fall due only at
the steps that carry them.

## Run notes

{{RUN_NOTES}}

---

Begin your control loop. You have the whole process above before the first
step opens -- read it, work out what must be watched and for how long, and put
in place what is already in force. Contracts are static per-object reference
data: fetch each once and keep it. The event stream is what tells you where
the run has got to. Monitor to completion.

Evaluate what is due at each step's end, but do not record a verdict for a
step that checked out clean -- hold its clause results, tagged with its
step_id, in working memory. Record a verdict only on a finding: a violation
you caught as it happened and adjudicated, or an end_step check that failed a
clause nothing had caught, scoped to the step it happened in and recorded the
moment you have it. If the whole run produces no finding at all, call
record_run_verdict exactly once at the end, covering every step together.

The process above is what is expected, not a limit on what you will see. A
step whose step_id is not on it is unplanned: it is governed by every
requirement already in force, judged like any other step, and you must name it
as unplanned in whatever you record.
